1. AI Market Trends

Meta says it has reached the frontier. ✅ Meta released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full on Monday, October 12. Both have a context window of about 1 million tokens and accept text, image, audio and video input with text output. Muse Wonder is available through the Meta Model API at $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, and through Muse Code, Meta's coding agent. Muse Wonder Full is not on the API and comes with Muse Code subscriptions at $50 or $200 a month. Both are proprietary, with no downloadable weights (Tech Times, October 13, citing VentureBeat; Decrypt, October 12). ✅ In Meta's own table, Muse Wonder Full scores 57.5 on SWE-Marathon against 46.3 for Claude Opus 5.5 and 42.5 for GPT-6 Astra; Decrypt reports that Meta acknowledges this is not a controlled comparison. The third-party Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index lists Claude Opus 5.5 at 58, Muse Wonder Full and Claude Sonnet 5.5 at 56, GPT-6 Astra at 53 and Muse Wonder at 52 (Decrypt; Tech Times; Meta results self-reported). ⚠️ Tech Times reports that rivals scored higher than Wonder Full on Terminal Bench and HealthBench Pro, that Muse Code subscriptions default to a mode that lets Meta use prompts, code and outputs to improve future models, and that a full safety report is still to come. We read secondary write-ups, not Meta's announcement.

Microsoft pushes inference onto its own silicon. ✅ Microsoft announced Maia 300 on October 12. As reported, it is built mainly for inference, uses eight HBM4 memory stacks with 18 TB/s of peak memory bandwidth, and sits in a rack of 96 accelerators. Satya Nadella said it "delivers 40% more tokens per watt compared to leading GPU-based systems." Microsoft plans multi-gigawatt Maia deployments starting next calendar year and will keep using Nvidia and AMD chips (Business Today, October 13, 2026, citing Nadella's post and Microsoft's announcement). ⚠️ All performance figures are Microsoft's; the article names no baseline and cites no independent testing.

Decart: a conflict resolved, a deal still unconfirmed. 🔶 Reuters, relaying the Wall Street Journal and citing unnamed sources, reported on October 11 that Amazon is in talks to acquire Decart for around $7 billion, that a deal could come soon unless talks collapse or another bidder appears, and that Anthropic explored a deal of about $6 billion in August but decided last month not to proceed after due diligence (Reuters, via KFGO, October 11, 2026). ✅ A September 8 summary of a Bloomberg report says the same about Anthropic. This replaces the earlier "Anthropic nears deal" report from August as the more recent account (The Deep Dive, September 8, 2026, citing Bloomberg). ⚠️ Neither company has confirmed anything; we read syndicated and secondhand copies, not the WSJ or Bloomberg originals. "Around $7 billion" is a sourced approximation.

Nvidia and Reflection AI, still headline-only. 🔶 Nvidia's quote page lists "Nvidia in talks to either acquire, increase investment in Reflection AI, FT say" (TheFly) and "Nvidia Looks to Purchase Open-Weight AI Firm Reflection" (PYMNTS) (stockanalysis.com headline list, October 13). ⚠️ We opened none of them; treat the existence of a bid as unconfirmed.

Safety posture from a platform CEO. ✅ Satya Nadella urged companies to assume a powerful model could be compromised, to build mechanisms that let authorized staff pause or shut down a model mid-task, to avoid depending on a single model for critical decisions, and to keep tamper-proof records of agent actions (Morung Express, citing IANS, October 11, 2026). ⚠️ The article is a wire rewrite of Nadella's remarks and an X post; we did not read the original.

Anthropic IPO timing, still secondhand. 🔶 Search results list Bloomberg-sourced items headlined "Anthropic Plans IPO Investor Meetings as Mega-Listing Nears" and "Anthropic Said to Set October 14 Pre-IPO Investor Day, Targeting November Listing" (Bloomberg Tax and iTiger, as listed). ⚠️ We read only the headlines. Anthropic has not announced a date, price range or ticker that we could find.

Carried, not re-verified. ✅ Anthropic's updated Usage Policy takes effect November 12; Anthropic committed $150 million over three years to the Genesis Mission (Anthropic, October 8, company-reported).
