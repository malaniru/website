1. AI Market Trends

Google Cloud puts a multi-model agent in front of enterprise systems. ✅ Reuters describes the Gemini agent as a single AI agent for work that answers questions, handles tasks, creates content and writes code, plans work, uses tools and connects to a company's business systems. It runs inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Calendar and can also be reached through Microsoft 365 and Slack. For each task it selects a model, currently Google's Gemini and Anthropic's Claude, with more to come. Users can create "coworker agents" that have their own email address and can only reach information they are given.
