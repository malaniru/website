What This Means for You. The two releases point at the same question from opposite ends, and the pairing is what makes them useful. ✅ Anthropic's disclosure establishes that a serious agent deployment is a monitored deployment — 100% pre-execution coverage, a measured block rate, a bounded human-review queue — and it does so with numbers you can point at rather than principles you can nod at.
