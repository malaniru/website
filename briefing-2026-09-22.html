1. AI Market Trends

The AI trade's centre of gravity moved offshore for a day, and it moved because a company answered a policy argument with a capital-expenditure schedule. ✅ Alibaba's Apsara Conference produced three commitments in one morning: silicon, capacity and model scale (press, multi-source). ✅ The Zhenwu V900 is positioned as a domestic substitute at the high end — three times the M890 launched in May, described by Eddie Wu as the most powerful AI chip in China today, entering mass production in Q1 2027. ✅ The capacity number is the one that will outlast the chip headline: more than 20 gigawatts of Alibaba Cloud data-centre capacity by 2032. ✅ And the model roadmap is explicitly a scaling bet — Qwen 4 in training now, Qwen 4.5 and Qwen 5 projected at 5 to 10 trillion parameters, against 2.4 trillion for the current Qwen 3.8 Max flagship.
