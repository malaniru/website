What This Means for You. Monday's pullback changes nothing about the deployable stack: Opus 5 as the load-bearing default with an effort setting, GPT-5.6 as the mature competitor, Kimi K3 as a benchmarked open-weight base, with DeepSeek V4-Flash-0731 worth a price-performance look for cost-sensitive, non-sensitive workloads. The MCP migration remains the concrete engineering task — exercise every connector against the new Tier 1 SDKs, confirm nothing depends on the removed handshake or session header, and flag Enterprise-Managed Authorization for sensitive-data work. The market's Monday worry — the capital-intensity and circular-financing of the AI buildout — is exactly the concentration-and-dependency risk a documented AI implementation policy plus hands-on training are meant to name: they keep your stack legible when the vendors it rests on wobble. Anthropic's IPO roadshow and Wednesday's CPI are tracking items, not changes to delivery.
