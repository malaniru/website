The general transparency obligations applied from August 2, 2026: people must be told when they are interacting with an AI (chatbots, voice assistants, AI agents), and AI-generated synthetic audio, image, video, and text must be marked machine-readable and detectable. The obligations apply to all in-scope systems regardless of market date, but the AI Omnibus provisional agreement (May 2026) gives generative systems already on the market before August 2 until December 2, 2026 to meet the machine-readable marking requirement (Article 50(2)). Penalties can reach €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (Source: Cooley, "EU AI Act: Transparency Obligations Take Effect 2 August 2026," August 3; Sidley Data Matters; European Commission digital-strategy, 2026 — legal analysis/primary). ⚠️ Compliance awareness, not legal advice; confirm applicability with counsel.

The EU transparency duties are in force — chatbots and AI agents must disclose they are AI, and synthetic content must be marked — while the heavier machine-readable content-marking obligation for pre-existing generative systems runs to December 2.
