Meanwhile US markets reopen this morning with futures higher and chips bid as oil sinks, the MCP stateless spec goes final tomorrow, and the EU AI Act's August 2 transparency date sits six days out. The enterprise-agent adoption picture is still a governance-gap story. The through-line holds: deployment is widespread, trusted production is not, and the bottleneck is operational governance more than model capability.

What This Means for You. For organizations adopting AI, today's two events change the talking points but not the deployable stack: Opus 4.8 and Sonnet 5 behind MCP connectors held steady through the entire drawdown-rebound-earnings cycle, and that stability is worth more than any single-week market move. Gemini's continued absence after a rebuild is the clearest available reminder that "announced" and "assessable" are different states, and a model-selection policy should encode the difference.

3. Agentic AI Advances & New Releases

  • Kimi K3 — open weights out (early), and now benchmarked. 2.8T-parameter MoE, ~50B active per token, native multimodal, 1M context; MXFP4 weights with a ~1.4TB footprint. Independent evaluation places it #1 on LMArena's Frontend Code Arena (1,679 Elo), at Intelligence Index 57 (#4 overall, strongest open model), with reported coding scores including 88.3% Terminal-Bench 2.1, 81.2% FrontierSWE, 67.5% DeepSWE, 42.0% SWE Marathon. Together AI and Modal shipped day-0 hosting (Source: VentureBeat; wan27.org; emergent.sh; Northflank, July 2026 — press/aggregator). ⚠️ Scores mix KimiCode, Claude Code, Codex and mini-SWE-agent harnesses; harness choice can swing results 10–26 points, and Moonshot concedes overall UX still trails the strongest proprietary models.
  • MCP 2026-07-28 — final tomorrow. Stateless core (SEP-2575 removes the initialize handshake; SEP-2567 removes the Mcp-Session-Id header and protocol-level session), Extensions framework (MCP Apps server-rendered UIs, long-running Tasks), OAuth/OIDC-aligned authorization, and a formal deprecation policy. A server that needed sticky sessions and a shared session store can now run behind a plain round-robin load balancer and route on an Mcp-Method header; the 10-week validation window (May 21 → July 28) closes tomorrow, and beta Python, TypeScript, Go and C# SDKs shipped (Source: modelcontextprotocol.io blog; Microsoft Tech Community, "MCP Just Went Stateless"; WorkOS; The Register — primary/press).
  • DeepSeek legacy aliases retired Friday. deepseek-chat and deepseek-reasoner stopped resolving at 15:59 UTC on July 24 — a hard cutoff, no fallback. Migrated calls use deepseek-v4-flash or deepseek-v4-pro; same base URL, key and request shape, but v4-flash defaults to thinking on, so pin thinking: disabled to reproduce the old non-thinking behavior (Source: DeepSeek API docs; Enterprise DNA; TECHi, July 2026 — primary/press).
  • Claude — Add-ins and Copilot reach (carry). A new Add-ins capability lets Claude operate directly inside productivity software (e.g., Microsoft Word) rather than through a connector, and Microsoft 365 Copilot has added Claude and Cowork agent workflows (Source: Windows Forum; Releasebot, July 2026 — press/secondary).
  • Google shipped Flash, not Pro (carry). The July 21 batch — Gemini 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, and a Flash "Cyber" variant — landed, but Gemini 3.5 Pro is still not out; Google's position is "testing with partners," no date (Source: TechCrunch, July 21; CroeAi, July 2026 — press).

What This Means for You. The dated events that touch live deployments are now landing, not pending. MCP is the immediate one — one day to final — so any connector built against the initialize handshake or protocol session IDs should be exercised against the beta SDKs today, before Tuesday; this is exactly the integration-hygiene work worth putting on a calendar. Kimi K3 is a ready-made trial case: day-0 hosting on Together/Modal means a team can evaluate a frontier-class open model without a ~1.4TB build-out, which turns "open weights" into a data-residency and governance conversation rather than an infrastructure one. The Cowork Add-ins surface still warrants a note that working "inside" an app changes the data-handling story versus a connector, and the Gemini rebuild remains the reminder that evaluation harnesses beat announcement-watching.
