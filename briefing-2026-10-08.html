1. AI Market Trends

Anthropic introduces Claude Haiku 5.5. ✅ The October 7 post lists pricing of $0.10 input and $0.50 output per million tokens for prompts up to 100,000 tokens ($0.50 and $2.50 above that), cache reads at $0.01 ($0.05 above 100,000), and says Haiku 5.5 costs "around 75% less to run" on average than Haiku 4.5; it is available on Anthropic's API, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure under the model ID claude-haiku-5-5, and is the first Haiku-class model with an adjustable effort setting (Anthropic, October 7, 2026, company-reported). 🔶 The Claude Code changelog entry for v2.1.293 gives a 1M-token context window; the product page we read did not state a context window, so we attribute that figure to the changelog only. ⚠️ All benchmark figures in the post are Anthropic's own (self-reported — independent verification unavailable); see Section 3.

Anthropic relaunches its Cyber Verification Program. ✅ The October 6 post says the program now has three tiers (Defense Access, Red Team Access and Specialized Access), merges the earlier Project Glasswing and Cyber Verification trusted-access programs, and requires data retention so Anthropic can monitor for cyber misuse (Anthropic, October 6, 2026, company-reported; page last modified October 7). ✅ The post reports that partners uncovered "at least 129,000 verified software vulnerabilities" between April and July 2026 and that Anthropic's open-source scanning found 5,500 more between April and October 2026 (Anthropic, company-reported). ⚠️ These are vendor figures we could not check against an independent source.

OpenAI puts GPT-6 and an Intelligent UI in front of ChatGPT users. ✅ The October 7 post says GPT-6 Sol powers Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise, GPT-6 Luna powers Free and Go, and responses can include graphics, buttons, forms, charts and interactive tools; it cites "more than 1.2 billion people who use ChatGPT every week" and says GPT-6 Instant starts answering 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant on web-search questions (OpenAI Newsroom, October 7, 2026, company-reported). ⚠️ Self-reported — independent verification unavailable. The post does not mention pricing.

OpenAI expands ChatGPT for Teens and funds a nonprofit. ✅ The October 7 post says OpenAI is supporting the College Advising Corps, a nonprofit that places advisers in under-resourced high schools, and a three-year Student Advisory Council at Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab; it also reports that teens average under 15 minutes a day on ChatGPT (OpenAI Newsroom, October 7, 2026, company-reported). ⚠️ Usage figures are OpenAI's own.
